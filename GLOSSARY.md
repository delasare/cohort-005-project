# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. A purchase can have at most one Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet. The learner keeps access to the course while it is open.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it is the moment the learner loses access to the course. Their progress is kept.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a Decline Reason. It is final: it has no Refund, and the learner cannot raise another Refund Request for that purchase.

**Decline Reason**:
The Admin's explanation, shown to the learner, of why their Refund Request was declined.

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who decides Refund Requests. Not a course's instructor, and not an admin of a team.
_Avoid_: The team, Reviewer, Moderator

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
