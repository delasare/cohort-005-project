# A Refund ends access at approval but keeps progress

A Student loses access to a course the moment an Admin approves their Refund Request — not when they ask, and not when the money arrives — and their lesson progress, watch history and quiz attempts are kept rather than deleted. Ending access at the request would punish Students whose request is declined; waiting for the money would tie access to a payment flow that can lag or fail after the decision is already final. Keeping progress preserves the watch history that justified the decision if it is ever disputed, and lets a Student who buys again pick up where they left off.

## Consequences

- A Student keeps full access while their Refund Request waits, so they can keep watching; the Admin should judge on progress as it is now, not as it was when they asked.
- Buying the course again is a new Purchase: it restores access and may have its own Refund Request, but watch history carries over, so Refund Eligibility still applies.
